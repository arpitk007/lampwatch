One slow answer doesn’t page anyone

A site is called down only when it misses three checks in a row, and back up after two passes. That’s the difference between an alert you act on and one you learn to ignore.

  1. Three misses in a row. Timeouts, connection errors or a 5xx. One alone is noise, so nothing happens yet.
  2. An incident opens. Every alert channel gets a message: email, Telegram, or both, with the error that tripped it.
  3. Two passes close it. You get the recovery message with how long it lasted, and your status page says so too.

A status page your users can actually read

Thirty days of uptime per service and a plain sentence about what’s broken. It never shows your URLs or error messages.
